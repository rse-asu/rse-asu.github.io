About the working group

How are coding agents changing scientific computing, and what could ASU-RSE do to help researchers adapt their work? Come join us for a discussion of the current landscape, experiences with these tools, and possible actions and continuing initiatives for ASU-RSE.

This group is not limited to serving dedicated software engineers: we are interested to work with any interested scientists applying agentic AI for software that aids or extends their research.
