How are coding agents changing scientific computing, and what could ASU-RSE do to help researchers adapt their work? Come join us for a discussion of the current landscape, experiences with these tools, and possible actions and continuing initiatives for ASU-RSE.

**When?** Thursday, September 17, 2026, 10am to 11:30am MST, plus social time  
**Where?** Conference Room at ECA 120 ([link to Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/oz8EMDeWjiEquVfo8)), or online on [Zoom](https://asu.zoom.us/j/4569055304)

Agenda items:

- **The current landscape.** What does the state of coding agents in scientific computing actually look like?
- **Tools and experiences.** What are people encountering with coding agents, and why does it matter for scientific work?
- **What researchers need.** How is agent use affecting scientific measurement, interpretation, understanding, validation, collaboration, and reusability beyond the original project? (If we don't know, how do we find out?)
- **Possible actions this year.** What could ASU-RSE usefully do? One possibility is a collection of structured cases from research teams, beginning at ASU and designed to extend beyond it. What else?
- **What to carry forward, and by whom.** What can we reasonably take on, and should it sit with ASU-RSE, a subcommittee, or something else? We'll discuss initial responsibilities, first steps, communication with research teams, and how this work could help ASU-RSE.
