🚀 **Need Expert Feedback on Your Research Code or Project Idea?**  
ASU’s experienced Research Software Engineers (RSEs) are here to help! Research Software Engineering Services are offered by the Research Technology Office (RTO) at Arizona State University to support and accelerate computational research and research that involves the development of software. This service is delivered by a team of professional RSEs who bridge the gap between high quality software engineering and domain-specific academic research.

🔍 Our core capabilities include but are not limited to:\
✔ **Custom Software Development:** Building bespoke applications, data pipelines, and research tools designed around your specific project requirements.\
✔ **Architecture & Infrastructure Strategy:** Consulting on the planning, design, and infrastructure of complex computational workflows.\
✔ **Codebase Audits:** Evaluating existing code to maximize long-term sustainability, maintainability, and security.\
✔ **Project Management & Best Practices:** Advising on optimal project setups, workflows, and software engineering standards.\
✔ **Containerization & Deployment:** Packaging software using Docker or Singularity and setting up continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines for seamless reproducibility.\
✔ **Team Training & Workshops:** Conducting hands-on workshops for research teams on version control (Git), testing frameworks, documentation, and scientific programming skills.\
✔ **Legacy Code Modernization:** Migrating outdated codebases (e.g., legacy Python 2) to modern languages, frameworks, or cloud environments.\
✔ **Grant & Proposal Support:** Supporting grant proposal writing by contributing to technical specifications, data management plans, and software sustainability sections for funding applications.\
✔ **Open Source Development:** Guiding the creation of open-source research software, including setting up public repositories, and establishing code conventions and contribution guidelines to ensure long-term community adoption.\
